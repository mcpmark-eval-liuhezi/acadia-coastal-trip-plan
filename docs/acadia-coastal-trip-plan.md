## Coastal campgrounds

Both candidates were pulled from the National Park Service's official campground listing for Acadia National Park (parkCode `acad`), which returns four campgrounds in total. Blackwoods vs. Seawall is the head-to-head that decides our base.

### Blackwoods Campground
- **Location / access (per listing):** east side of Mount Desert Island, about 5 mi (8 km) south of Bar Harbor on Route 3 (Otter Creek; 155 Blackwoods Drive). All sites are wooded and within a 10-minute walk of the ocean. Paved roads — all vehicles OK; RVs and trailers allowed.
- **Season (per listing):** "Open seasonally, usually from early May to mid-October."
- **Sites:** 281 total, all reservable in advance (none first-come, first-served): 221 tent-only, 60 RV-only, 4 group. No electrical hookups.
- **Amenities (per listing):** trash/recycling collection; flush toilets (seasonal); potable water (seasonal); dump station; camp store; ice and firewood for sale; food storage lockers; laundry; amphitheater; staff/volunteer host onsite; internet connectivity; cell phone reception. No showers.
- **Access notes (per listing):** 12 accessible drive-in sites (RV non-electric and tent-only); RV sites max 35 ft (10.7 m); hard-packed walkways to wheelchair-accessible restrooms and the amphitheater.
- **Fees / booking:** $30/night tent-only or RV non-electric; $60/night group tent area; park entrance pass required in addition. Reservations via Recreation.gov only — 90% of sites released six months ahead on the first of each month at 10 am EST; the remaining 10% at 10 am 14 days before arrival (campground 232508).

### Seawall Campground
- **Location / access (per listing):** west side of Mount Desert Island, about 4 mi (6 km) south of Southwest Harbor, roughly 18 mi from Bar Harbor (664 Seawall Rd). All sites are within a short walking distance of the coastline. Paved roads — all vehicles OK; RVs and trailers allowed, 20 ft max.
- **Season (per listing):** "Open seasonally, typically from the Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day." Ranger station generally 9 am–8 pm in peak season, staffing-dependent.
- **Sites:** 202 total, all reservable: 43 tent-only, 59 RV-only, 98 walk-in, 5 group. No electrical hookups.
- **Amenities (per listing):** identical list to Blackwoods — trash/recycling collection; flush toilets (seasonal); potable water (seasonal); dump station; camp store; ice and firewood for sale; food storage lockers; laundry; amphitheater; staff/volunteer host onsite; internet connectivity; cell phone reception. No showers.
- **Access notes (per listing):** accessible sites include 3 RV, 5 drive-in, 5 walk-in, and one group site; accessible restroom in the C loop reached by a long, hard-packed ramp.
- **Fees / booking:** $30/night standard, RV, or tent-only non-electric; $22/night walk-in; $60/night group tent area. Same Recreation.gov release schedule as Blackwoods (campground 234674).

### Head-to-head

| Factor | Blackwoods | Seawall |
|---|---|---|
| Side of island | East; 5 mi south of Bar Harbor on Route 3 | West; 4 mi south of Southwest Harbor, ~18 mi from Bar Harbor |
| Coast access | All sites within a 10-minute walk of the ocean | All sites a short walking distance from the coastline |
| Sites | 281 (221 tent-only, 60 RV-only, 4 group) | 202 (43 tent-only, 59 RV-only, 98 walk-in, 5 group) |
| Amenities | Identical lists — no showers at either | Identical lists — no showers at either |
| RV / trailer limit | RV sites to 35 ft | RV/trailer max 20 ft |
| Nightly fee | $30 tent/RV; $60 group | $30 standard; $22 walk-in; $60 group |

**Season flag — decisive this year.** The listing's own seasonal dates put both campgrounds' close of season at or before our 24–25 October weekend: Seawall typically closes Columbus Day (Monday 12 October 2026) and Blackwoods usually runs only "early May to mid-October." On the listing as it stands, neither campground should be assumed open for this trip, and with amenities identical, the base decision collapses to availability. Confirm live availability with the park / Recreation.gov before anything else in this plan is fixed.

### Other coastal options in the listing (brief)
- **Duck Harbor Campground (Isle au Haut):** five lean-to shelter sites on a rugged island off Stonington, linked to the mainland by mailboat and closed to automobiles; the listing calls it "a great launch point for paddling excursions." $20/night, reservations released 10 am April 1 for the entire season, max 3 nights, six people per site, one stay per calendar year, no pets, pack-out trash. The most paddler-specific campground in the listing, but a different class of trip. The listing gives no operating season for it.
- **Schoodic Woods Campground (Schoodic Peninsula):** the listing's newest campground, 1.5 mi southeast of Winter Harbor and roughly 60–70 minutes from Bar Harbor; 89 reservable sites (78 with electrical hookups), $30 standard electric / $40 RV electric-and-water / $22 hike-in / $60 group; open from the Wednesday before Memorial Day weekend until Columbus Day. Worth flagging because it sits beside our second marine checkpoint — though the same Columbus Day close applies.

## Shuttle access

- **Status for our dates: not running.** The Island Explorer's season typically runs late June through mid-October, and retrieved web sources report the 2026 regular seasonal service ended 12 October 2026. Our 24–25 October weekend falls after it — do not build logistics around the shuttle; plan private-vehicle shuttles.
- **Fare: free.** The Island Explorer is Acadia's fare-free, propane-powered shuttle connecting the park with Bar Harbor and other Mount Desert Island communities.
- **Campground reach (in season):** it has served Blackwoods Campground (a route was extended to include a Blackwoods stop), but western-side service is not guaranteed — recent reporting noted no bus service to Southwest Harbor, and Seawall is not reliably served. Scheduled stops vary by time of year and day.
- **Verify:** check islandexplorer.app or Downeast Transportation before the final plan is published, in case any late-season or special service exists.
